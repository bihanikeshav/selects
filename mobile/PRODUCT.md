# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Photographers and travellers making a first pass through selected photos on their phone. The user explicitly chose a standalone app that imports and processes photos on the phone, rather than a desktop companion.

## Purpose

Carry Selects' local-first review workflow onto Android. Surface useful technical signals, keep originals untouched, and leave every decision with the person reviewing the photos.

## Visual direction

Follow the existing practical, calm Material-style product. Use native typography, photo-led grids, restrained warm neutrals, and the system light or dark theme. People review outside during a trip and indoors later, so respect the device theme rather than forcing one ambient setting.

## Interaction principles

Use the system picker and selected-photo access. Show real processing state and recoverable errors. Save reviews locally. Keep and reject are explicit, reversible actions. Reprocessing preserves decisions and skips healthy analysis. Use text as well as color for state, minimum native touch targets, readable type, and descriptions for photo previews.

## Limits

Do not present technical scores as aesthetic understanding. No decorative onboarding, account requirement, or desktop connection step. The first development slice is image import, technical analysis, and persistent review.
