# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Photographers and travellers reviewing large folders of photos and video after a trip, on their own desktop or laptop.

## Product Purpose

Selects makes a local first pass through media, surfaces promising shots, and leaves the final decision to the person. Photos and analysis stay on the user's machine.

## Brand Personality

Practical, calm, direct. Follow the existing Material-style interface and plain language, as confirmed for the update feature.

## Anti-references

Avoid disruptive redesigns, promotional language, and interruptions to the review workflow. Reuse established navigation and controls.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the current interface and light/dark themes.
- Keep the user in control of network activity and downloads.
- Show accurate progress and recoverable errors.
- Make maintenance actions available without requiring a photo library.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, labelled controls, and status announcements. Do not rely on color alone to convey update status.
